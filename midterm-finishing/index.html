Line 1 Asks for a URL and a Date

The same URL gives you a different file next year. Statistical agencies revise: GDP is re-estimated, the World Bank updates its indicators more than once a year, and FRED keeps old versions of a series in ALFRED because the numbers change.

Your number belongs to one download. The URL says where; the date says which version.

Rubric, repository row: “A stranger who clones it should be able to get to your number without emailing you.” Line 1 is the start of that.

Draw It First: Which Controls, and Why

Confounders. Year and format point into both age and the score (age at the game: 34.3 before 2016, 26.9 after; live-action 31.9, animated 26.8). Control for them: each blocks a backdoor path.

Possible mediator. Director → budget → score. Leave it out; in these data it is not a mediator (-0.36 → -0.37).

Franchise: checked with a crude flag. 22 of 57 films follow an earlier film of the same game (reboots count; first films get 0). Adding the flag: age -0.30 → -0.30. The trust a studio put in a first director is still unmeasured.

Not required by the brief. It is the quickest way to write Section 3’s “role of every control” (Topic 10).

Reading the Section 4 Output

Residual = actual − predicted. A fan would mean the usual standard errors cannot be trusted; a curve, that a line is the wrong shape. Neither. Spread -36 to +47 (SD 19.4): most of a score is unexplained.
Name the points. Ringed at 13: the 3 Sonic films, 20 to 37 points above the model. At 1: The Super Mario Bros. Movie, 15 below.
Breusch–Pagan p = 0.33. No evidence of unequal variance, but with 57 films the test has little power: the plots matter more, and HC1 is reported anyway.
VIF 1.04–1.12. Age, year and format barely overlap, so none inflates another’s standard error. The wide interval comes from noisy scores, not collinearity.
Cook’s D: Resident Evil: Damnation 0.211. A score of 100% from 5 reviews: mostly noise. That is why Section 4 also re-estimates without the films under 20 reviews.
Add age²? R² always rises. AIC 503.6 → 504.3 and held-out error 20.24 → 21.15 both get slightly worse: the square is fitting noise. Keep the line.

Threats, Conclusion, and the Repository

Threats and conclusion · 10

X carries two things: a young director, or an old game adapted late (Mario, Street Fighter). And it is a stand-in for having played it.

Franchises: only a crude flag, which changes nothing. A cut nobody planned (under 20 at the game, 6 films) looks big and is franchise and timing. So the estimate is a conditional association, small and imprecise.

Another month: directors’ own gaming history, who the studio considered, audience scores.
